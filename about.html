Jhoda Edquist

From the Dealership Floor to Your Corner

Jhoda has worked every role in a dealership sales department—from salesperson to General Manager—while setting sales and client-satisfaction records along the way. He later brought that real-world experience into corporate automotive, leading national sales campaigns and helping launch programs including Direct-to-Consumer models and Executive Concierge Purchasing.

He knows how the industry works because he has worked inside it. Now, he puts that knowledge to work for you.

Why Curated Whips Exists

Buying a vehicle should feel exciting—not confusing, pressured, or uncertain.

After helping thousands of clients across the country, Jhoda repeatedly saw consumers facing the same uphill battle: limited access to reliable pricing data, dealership tactics that have become normal, and no clear way to tell whether they are actually getting a fair deal.

For years, he helped friends and family cut through the noise, negotiate with confidence, and find the right vehicle at the right price. They encouraged him to bring that same advantage to everyone.

That is why Curated Whips exists: to level the playing field, remove the stress, and help you feel confident from your first search to the moment you drive away.

Your Advocate—Not the Dealer's

Curated Whips works for one person: you.

We are not loyal to any dealership. We do not accept dealer payments, referral fees, or broker-style commissions that can create conflicts of interest. Our advice, research, sourcing, and negotiation strategy are built around your needs, your budget, and your best outcome.

You deserve more than a salesperson's pitch. You deserve an experienced automotive professional in your corner.
