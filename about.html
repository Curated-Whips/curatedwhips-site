About

Decades on the dealership floor. Now working for you.

Jhoda Edquist

Owner & Operator, Curated Whips

From Dealership Floor to Corporate Auto

Jhoda has worked every position in a dealership sales department — from salesperson to General Manager — setting several sales and client satisfaction records along the way. In the corporate automotive space, he's led sales campaigns and built new programs like Direct-to-Consumer models and Concierge Purchasing for executives.

Why Curated Whips Exists

After helping thousands of clients nationwide with their auto needs, Jhoda saw the same problem over and over: the average consumer is outmatched — limited access to trustworthy data, dealer tactics that have become normalized, and no real way to know what a good deal looks like. After years of helping friends and family navigate this, they convinced him to make the service available to everyone. Curated Whips was built to give clients that same edge — and get back to enjoying the car-buying process.

Our Commitment to You

Honesty, authenticity, and a genuine desire to exceed expectations drive everything we do. We have no loyalty or affiliation to any dealer, we accept no payments like most auto brokers do, and we act only in your best interests.
